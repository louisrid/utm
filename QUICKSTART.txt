FANVUE UTM TRACKER - QUICKSTART
===============================
Your original Google Sheet is the only place to add/edit placements.
Current scope: FANVUE X ARTICLE TRACKER, including Louis + Connor tabs.

1. Unzip the project on your Mac.
2. In Terminal, cd into the fanvue-utm-tracker folder.
3. Run:
   npx netlify-cli login
   npx netlify-cli deploy --prod
4. Copy the new https://YOUR-SITE.netlify.app URL.
5. Open https://script.google.com/home/projects/create
6. Paste the whole apps-script/Code.gs file.
7. Change SITE_URL near the top to your real Netlify URL.
8. Save and run the setup function; approve Google permissions.
9. Copy the APP_SCRIPT_TOKEN from the Execution log.
10. Deploy Apps Script > New deployment > Web app > Execute as Me > Access Anyone.
11. In Netlify site settings, add:
    APP_SCRIPT_URL=<Google Web app /exec URL>
    APP_SCRIPT_TOKEN=<log token>
    DASHBOARD_KEY=<your chosen dashboard password>
12. Deploy Netlify again: npx netlify-cli deploy --prod
13. Open the dashboard; log in with DASHBOARD_KEY.
14. Open your existing Sheet: columns O:P now contain permanent IDs and UTM links.
15. Check click logging by opening a link; run refreshReports once to see first totals.

New publisher rows are detected automatically via the installable edit trigger.
Every 12 hours click totals and dashboard summary refresh automatically.

NOTE: A ZIP drag-and-drop to Netlify as a static site will not deploy
serverless Functions. Use the CLI commands above or a Git-backed Netlify project.

Full details, troubleshooting and caveats are in README.md.
